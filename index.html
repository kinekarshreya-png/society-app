About this App

Society Expense Portal

A digital ledger built specifically for housing societies to manage, track, and report expenses — transparently and efficiently.

📸

Bill Uploads

Capture bills directly with camera or upload from gallery. Auto-compressed for storage.

🗂

Categories

Electricity, Water, Salaries, Maintenance and 15+ expense heads built in.

📊

Charts & Analytics

Monthly bar charts, category-wise pie charts, and trend views per society.

📁

Excel Export

One-tap export of all bills to Excel — ready for audits and meetings.

🔒

Secure Access

Each society has their own login. Admin approves all accounts.

🏘

Multi-Society

Admin can manage unlimited societies from one dashboard.
